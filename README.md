## ✨ Features
1. 🤖 **Minimax AI Opponent:** Play against an intelligent AI with three difficulty tiers:
  - **Easy:** Mostly random moves, occasionally blocks wins.
  - **Medium:** A mix of strategic and random moves (60/40 split).
  - **Hard:** Uses the Minimax algorithm with alpha-beta pruning. It is mathematically unbeatable!
2. 👥 **2-Player Mode:** Switch seamlessly to a local PvP match.
3. 🎨 **Dynamic UI:** Dark premium theme with glowing accents, animated mark drawing (X and O), and ambient floating particles.
4. 📐 **SVG Win Animations:** A dynamic line strikes through the winning combination.
5. 📊 **Persistent Score Tracking:** Keeps track of X, O, and Draw scores across multiple rounds.
6. 📱 **Responsive Design:** Optimized for all screen sizes.

## 🚀 How to Play
1. **Open** `index.html` in your web browser.
2. Select your mode: **vs AI** or **2 Players**.
3. If playing AI, choose your difficulty level.
4. Click or tap an empty cell to place your mark.
5. Get 3 in a row (horizontally, vertically, or diagonally) to win!
